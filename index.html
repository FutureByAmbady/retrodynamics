01 / WHO WE ARE

CURIOSITY, WITH PURPOSE.

ENGINEERING
IDEAS INTO
REALITY.

Curiosity, with purpose.

Retro Dynamics is an emerging engineering, robotics and technology initiative built around practical learning, innovation and product development.

We believe engineering knowledge becomes meaningful when ideas are tested, systems are explored and concepts are transformed into working solutions.

Our primary focus is engineering education and technical learning. Alongside this, we explore robotic product development and customised engineering projects.

OUR MISSION

Make engineering more practical, accessible and impactful.

OUR VISION

Develop a platform where people learn engineering by building, experimenting and innovating.

From concept to interaction. Alongside education, we aim to design and develop intelligent robotic systems, with more robotic technologies to explore in the future.

INITIAL FLAGSHIP INITIATIVE

Welcome &
Hospitality Robot

PRODUCT-DEVELOPMENT INITIATIVE

A mobile humanoid-style robot intended to welcome guests, assist at events, carry serving trays and create interactive experiences in hospitality environments.

This is an initial product-development direction, not a completed or commercially available robot. Its design and capabilities will evolve through prototyping and testing.
